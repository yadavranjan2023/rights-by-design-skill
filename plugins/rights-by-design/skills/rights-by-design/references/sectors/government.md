# Government and Public-Services Sector Module

## Scope

Apply to public benefits, taxation, licensing, permitting, education administration, immigration, policing support, inspections, fraud detection, child welfare, public housing, emergency management, public employment, and other exercises of public authority.

## Decision and harm model

Government AI can affect legal rights, liberty, property, public benefits, civic participation, family integrity, access to essential services, and public trust. Public systems carry heightened duties of legality, fairness, explanation, records preservation, and democratic accountability.

## Rights-specific review

### Privacy and cognitive liberty

Assess:

- statutory or authorized purpose;
- data sharing across agencies;
- surveillance and location data;
- biometrics;
- social-media and relationship inference;
- retention;
- secondary use;
- vendor access;
- chilling effects on speech, association, and civic participation.

### Due process and dignity

Verify:

- timely notice;
- legal and factual basis;
- access to relevant evidence;
- correction;
- meaningful opportunity to be heard;
- pre-deprivation process where required;
- qualified and impartial human review;
- accessible procedures;
- preservation of benefits or status where appropriate during appeal.

### Equal protection and nondiscrimination

Require:

- representative data;
- subgroup and geographic testing;
- disability and language access;
- analysis of enforcement and investigation burdens;
- community impact;
- intersectional effects;
- monitoring of referrals, flags, denials, sanctions, and appeals;
- public remediation procedures.

### Transparency and contestability

Provide:

- public system description;
- understandable individual notice;
- explanation of the system's role;
- input and evidence access subject to lawful limits;
- correction;
- hearing or appeal;
- authority to reverse;
- response timelines;
- public reporting proportionate to risk.

### Accountability and democratic control

Name agency owner, program owner, legal authority, data steward, vendor owner, audit authority, appeals authority, and official with suspension power.

Address procurement transparency, records retention, audit access, legislative or oversight review, public complaints, impact reporting, and vendor accountability.

## Sector controls

- documented legal authority and purpose;
- algorithmic or system impact assessment;
- public notice and documentation;
- records and audit plan;
- accessible individual notice;
- hearing, correction, and appeal;
- vendor transparency and audit rights;
- community and stakeholder review;
- policy and model-change approval;
- independent oversight;
- suspension and termination authority.

## Evidence required

- legal-authority memorandum;
- program rules;
- impact assessment;
- data-sharing agreements;
- notice and appeal materials;
- accessibility review;
- subgroup and geographic results;
- procurement terms;
- audit logs;
- complaint and appeal outcomes;
- public reporting plan;
- suspension procedure.

## Deployment blockers

- no identified legal authority;
- benefits, status, or rights altered without meaningful process;
- inaccessible notice or appeal;
- no ability to correct government data;
- covert use in consequential decisions;
- vendor secrecy preventing audit;
- no public or independent accountability;
- no official authorized to suspend the system.
