# Transportation Sector Module

## Scope

Apply to automated and assisted driving, rail and transit automation, aviation decision-support, fleet routing and dispatch, driver monitoring, and passenger-screening or fare-enforcement systems.

## Decision and harm model

Transportation AI can cause death or serious injury to passengers, workers, and vulnerable road users, and raises acute accountability questions after incidents. Perception and decision systems can perform unevenly across road-user groups, and cabin, location, and screening data are sensitive.

## Rights-specific review

### Privacy and cognitive liberty
Assess cabin, location, and movement data; minimize and purpose-limit to safety; and bar repurposing or monetization without consent.

### Due process and dignity
Where screening or fare-enforcement flags individuals, verify notice, human review, and recourse; ensure victims can obtain incident data.

### Equal protection and nondiscrimination
Test perception and any passenger-screening for disparate performance across demographics, mobility aids, and body types; remediate gaps.

### Transparency and contestability
Document a bounded operational design domain and a validated fallback; make the safety case legible to regulators and enable independent review.

### Accountability and democratic control
Provide tamper-evident event recording and defined investigator/victim access; name a safety owner; prevent foreseeable misuse of partial automation.

## Sector controls

- documented operational design domain and validated minimal-risk fallback;
- perception and screening equity testing;
- tamper-evident event data with investigator/victim access;
- human-factors design against partial-automation misuse;
- cabin/location data minimization and purpose limits;
- incident-response and reconstruction process;
- named safety owner and stop/withdrawal authority.

## Evidence required

- safety case and ODD documentation;
- fallback validation;
- stratified perception/screening performance;
- event-data-recorder design and access policy;
- driver-monitoring or human-factors validation;
- data map for cabin/location data.

## Presumptive deployment blockers

- undocumented ODD or unvalidated fallback for on-road/on-track operation;
- untested perception or screening equity;
- no investigator/victim access to incident data;
- foreseeable partial-automation misuse unaddressed.
