# Insurance Sector Module

## Scope

Apply to underwriting and pricing, claims triage and denial, special-investigation referral, and fraud scoring, including models that use alternative and third-party data.

## Decision and harm model

Insurance decisions gate access to essential financial protection and can encode protected-class proxies through alternative data. Generic adverse-action reasons defeat contestability, and claims triage that delays or denies can cause serious harm before a human intervenes.

## Rights-specific review

### Privacy and cognitive liberty
Inventory alternative and third-party data; identify inferred health, behavior, or protected traits; minimize and bar prohibited proxies.

### Due process and dignity
Verify human review and appeal for declines and claim denials, and that automated denials do not stand without meaningful review.

### Equal protection and nondiscrimination
Test underwriting and pricing for disparate impact; search for and document less-discriminatory alternatives.

### Transparency and contestability
Provide specific, accurate adverse-action reasons and an accessible appeal, not a generic letter.

### Accountability and democratic control
Require independent model validation, documentation, and scheduled fairness and performance monitoring.

## Sector controls

- feature governance barring prohibited proxies;
- disparate-impact and less-discriminatory-alternative testing;
- specific adverse-action reasons;
- human review and appeal for declines and denials;
- independent validation and monitoring;
- inference inventory and minimization for alternative data.

## Evidence required

- fairness-test and less-discriminatory-alternative records;
- reason-code specification;
- human-review workflow;
- feature-justification documentation;
- independent validation and monitoring plan.

## Presumptive deployment blockers

- untested proxy discrimination in pricing or underwriting;
- generic adverse-action reasons that prevent contestation;
- automated declines or denials with no human review;
- alternative data inferring protected traits without justification.
