# Predeployment Checklist

- [ ] Purpose and scope approved
- [ ] Prohibited uses documented
- [ ] Impact tier approved
- [ ] Sector overlays completed
- [ ] Data inventory and lineage complete
- [ ] Privacy and inference review complete
- [ ] Validation and subgroup testing complete
- [ ] Human authority tested
- [ ] Notice, correction, appeal, and remedy operational
- [ ] Accessibility tested
- [ ] Logs and reconstruction tested
- [ ] Security and adversarial testing complete
- [ ] Safe state, rollback, or shutdown tested
- [ ] Monitoring thresholds and incident response approved
- [ ] Vendor and contract controls complete
- [ ] Residual risk accepted by authorized owner
