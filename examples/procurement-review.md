# Example Review: Public-Sector AI Procurement


> **Use note:** This is a representative example for training, implementation, and evaluation. It is not a legal determination, regulatory approval, safety certification, or assessment of a named organization.


## 1. Executive summary

A state agency is procuring an AI fraud-detection platform for public-benefits programs. Three vendors have submitted proposals with different levels of transparency, testing, and audit access.

This example demonstrates how Rights by Design is used before award.

## 2. Procurement objectives

- Preserve legal and program authority
- Avoid vendor lock-in
- Require evidence rather than marketing claims
- Protect affected recipients
- Ensure auditability, appeal support, and correction
- Retain agency suspension authority

## 3. Evaluation categories

| Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Rights and due process | 20% |
| Validation and subgroup evidence | 15% |
| Transparency and contestability | 15% |
| Accountability and auditability | 15% |
| Security and privacy | 10% |
| Technical performance | 10% |
| Change and incident management | 10% |
| Cost and implementation | 5% |

## 4. Mandatory evidence

- Intended and prohibited uses
- Data inventory and lineage
- Validation and subgroup performance
- Explanation method
- Appeal and correction support
- Human-control design
- Audit and reconstruction
- Security architecture
- Incident history
- Model-change procedures
- Subprocessor list
- Data deletion and exit plan

## 5. Mandatory contract terms

- Agency audit rights
- Data-use limitations
- Incident notification
- Model-change notice and approval
- Cooperation with hearings and appeals
- Records preservation
- Performance and rights thresholds
- Suspension and termination
- Transition assistance
- No vendor secrecy that prevents legal or public accountability

## 6. Award blockers

- No subgroup validation
- No meaningful audit rights
- No decision reconstruction
- Refusal to support appeals
- Uncontrolled model updates
- Broad secondary use of agency data
- No exit or data-deletion plan

## 7. Bottom line

**Do not award based on aggregate accuracy or price alone.** Award should be conditioned on enforceable evidence, audit, appeal, change-control, and termination rights.
