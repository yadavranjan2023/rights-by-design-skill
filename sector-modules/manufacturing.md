# Manufacturing Sector Module

## Scope

Apply to AI in industrial and process control, predictive maintenance, automated optical inspection and defect classification, yield optimization, and workforce productivity, monitoring, and scheduling on the factory floor.

## Decision and harm model

Manufacturing AI governs hazardous processes and monitors labor. Unsafe automated control can injure workers faster than they can react; productivity and monitoring systems can penalize disability, breaks, or protected activity and drive unfair discipline; and defect models carry asymmetric costs where false negatives ship unsafe product and false positives blame workers.

## Rights-specific review

### Privacy and cognitive liberty
Assess floor monitoring scope; minimize to task and safety purpose; and bar off-task or biometric inference without necessity.

### Due process and dignity
Verify human review and appeal before discipline based on productivity or defect-attribution scores.

### Equal protection and nondiscrimination
Test pace, idle, and productivity metrics for disparate impact on disability, age, and protected activity; provide accommodation.

### Transparency and contestability
Explain how workers are scored; provide a dispute path for defect attribution and adverse scores.

### Accountability and democratic control
Bound safety-critical actuation to functional-safety limits with interlocks, override, and a named stop authority; protect independent safety reporting from monitoring-based retaliation.

## Sector controls

- functional-safety bounds, interlocks, and emergency stop on control;
- segregation of AI advice from safety-critical actuation;
- human review and appeal for worker-facing scores;
- disparate-impact testing of productivity metrics;
- safety-weighted defect-error analysis with human adjudication of consequential calls;
- proportionate, disclosed monitoring;
- protected, independent safety-reporting channels.

## Evidence required

- functional-safety case and test results;
- override and stop-authority records;
- worker-review and appeal workflow;
- productivity-metric disparate-impact analysis;
- defect-model error analysis;
- monitoring-scope disclosure and minimization record.

## Presumptive deployment blockers

- unbounded safety-critical actuation without interlocks or override;
- disciplinary scoring without human review or appeal;
- productivity metrics untested for disparate impact;
- monitoring that penalizes protected activity or chills safety reporting.
