# Energy and Utilities Sector Module

## Scope

Apply to AI for grid balancing and control, demand response and dynamic pricing, outage prediction and restoration sequencing, automated disconnection for non-payment, and smart-meter analytics.

## Decision and harm model

Power and water are essential services; wrongful disconnection, deprioritized restoration, or unaffordable pricing can endanger life and livelihood, especially for medically dependent and low-income households. Grid-control automation carries physical-safety risk, and high-resolution consumption data reveals occupancy and behavior.

## Rights-specific review

### Privacy and cognitive liberty
Assess smart-meter interval data that reveals occupancy, appliances, and routines; minimize to billing/operations purpose; and limit secondary use and sharing.

### Due process and dignity
Verify technically enforced protection for medically vulnerable and protected customers, and human review before any automated disconnection.

### Equal protection and nondiscrimination
Test restoration sequencing and dynamic pricing for geographic and demographic disparity; add equity constraints.

### Transparency and contestability
Provide specific, contestable reasons for disconnection, deposits, and pricing, and an expedited appeal with restoration authority.

### Accountability and democratic control
Bound grid-control autonomy with verified operator override; log decisions; and name a suspension authority.

## Sector controls

- medical-baseline and protected-customer disconnection safeguards;
- human review before disconnection;
- meter-data minimization and secondary-use limits;
- disparity testing and equity constraints on restoration and pricing;
- specific reasons and expedited, restoration-capable appeal;
- bounded, fail-safe grid autonomy with operator override;
- audit logging and a named stop authority.

## Evidence required

- vulnerable-customer protection rule and exception handling;
- pre-disconnection review workflow and logs;
- meter-data map and access logs;
- restoration and pricing disparity analysis;
- grid-control safety case and override tests;
- appeal SLAs and restoration records.

## Presumptive deployment blockers

- automated disconnection without enforced vulnerable-customer protection or human review;
- untested restoration or pricing equity;
- grid autonomy without verified operator override;
- meter data repurposed without consent;
- no authority to pause disconnection or grid automation.
