# The Five Rights-by-Design Dimensions

## Privacy and cognitive liberty
Protect personal information, behavioral inference, mental autonomy, and freedom from unjustified surveillance or manipulation.

## Due process and algorithmic dignity
Provide notice, reasons, factual correction, meaningful human judgment, and a real opportunity to be heard.

## Equal protection and nondiscrimination
Test for unequal treatment, unequal impact, inaccessible design, proxy effects, and unequal error burdens.

## Transparency and contestability
Make AI involvement understandable and provide correction, challenge, review, reversal, and remedy.

## Accountability and democratic control
Assign authority and responsibility, preserve traceability, govern vendors and changes, and maintain independent oversight and shutdown power.
