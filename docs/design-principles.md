# Design Principles

1. **Build before harm.** Protect rights in architecture and process before deployment.
2. **Evaluate the whole system.** Models do not act alone.
3. **Consequences determine impact.** Product labels do not.
4. **Evidence outranks assertion.** Policies and promises are not operational proof.
5. **Human review must have authority.** A rubber stamp is not control.
6. **Transparency must enable action.** Explanation without correction or remedy is incomplete.
7. **Fairness is measured in outcomes.** Removing protected attributes is not enough.
8. **Accountability requires a name and power.** Someone must be able to correct and stop the system.
9. **Sector context matters.** Universal rights remain constant; operational controls vary.
10. **Residual risk must be explicit.** Uncertainty should not be hidden behind a score.
