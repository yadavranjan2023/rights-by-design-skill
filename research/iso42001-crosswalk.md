# Rights by Design to ISO/IEC 42001 Crosswalk

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AI management system.

This document is a non-authoritative thematic crosswalk and does not reproduce ISO text.

| Rights by Design | AI management-system application |
|---|---|
| Impact tier and scope | Organizational context, AI system scope, risk and opportunity prioritization |
| Universal dimensions | Inputs to AI impact and risk assessment |
| Accountable owners | Leadership, roles, responsibilities, and authorities |
| Evidence model | Documented information and operational evidence |
| Build-in requirements | Objectives, controls, operational planning, and treatment |
| Monitoring and regression tests | Performance evaluation, internal audit, and management review |
| Incidents and remedy | Nonconformity, corrective action, and continual improvement |
| Sector overlays | Context-specific control selection and impact assessment |

## Certification warning

Only an accredited or otherwise qualified audit process can determine conformity or certification. Rights by Design can support readiness by identifying evidence and rights-centered control gaps.
