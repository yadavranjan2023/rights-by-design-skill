# Standards Crosswalk Overview

This file maps conceptual alignment. It is not a compliance determination.

| Rights by Design | NIST AI RMF | ISO/IEC 42001 themes | EU AI Act themes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Privacy and cognitive liberty | Map impacts; Measure privacy and harmful bias; Manage risks | Context, risk assessment, data governance, impact assessment | Data governance, transparency, fundamental-rights considerations |
| Due process and dignity | Govern accountability; Map affected parties; Manage impacts | Roles, objectives, impact assessment, operational controls | Human oversight, information to deployers, rights protections |
| Equal protection | Measure fairness and harmful bias; Map impacts | Risk and impact assessment, monitoring, corrective action | Data quality, risk management, accuracy, monitoring |
| Transparency and contestability | Govern documentation; Map context; Measure explainability | Documentation, communication, operation, monitoring | Transparency, instructions, record keeping, human oversight |
| Accountability and democratic control | Govern; continuous Map-Measure-Manage | Management-system leadership, roles, audit, corrective action | Provider/deployer duties, quality management, logs, post-market monitoring |

## Important limitation
ISO standards are copyrighted. This crosswalk summarizes high-level themes and does not reproduce the standard or establish certification readiness.
