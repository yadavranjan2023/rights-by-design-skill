# Rights by Design to the EU Artificial Intelligence Act Crosswalk

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 is in force. Obligations and application dates are phased, and the regulation should be reviewed in its current consolidated form for any specific use.

This crosswalk is informational and not legal advice.

| Rights by Design | EU AI Act alignment themes |
|---|---|
| Impact tier | Risk classification and use-context analysis |
| Prohibited and bounded uses | Prohibited practices and defined intended purpose |
| Data and equality review | Data governance, relevance, representativeness, and bias considerations |
| Human control test | Human oversight requirements |
| Evidence and traceability | Technical documentation, record keeping, logs, quality management |
| Accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity | Performance and resilience obligations |
| Notice and transparency | Transparency and information duties |
| Monitoring and incidents | Post-market monitoring and serious-incident processes |
| Government and rights review | Fundamental-rights impact considerations for applicable deployers and uses |

## Distinctive contribution

Rights by Design adds a structured due-process, contestability, remedy, cognitive-liberty, and democratic-control analysis that should be considered even where a system is not legally categorized as high risk.

## Verification note

Determine provider, deployer, importer, distributor, product-manufacturer, or other role; territorial scope; system classification; exclusions; and applicable dates before drawing legal conclusions.
