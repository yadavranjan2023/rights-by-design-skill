# Rights by Design to NIST AI RMF 1.0 Crosswalk

NIST AI RMF 1.0 is a voluntary, rights-preserving, non-sector-specific framework organized around **Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage**.

| Rights by Design requirement | Primary AI RMF function | Implementation use |
|---|---|---|
| Named accountable owner and stop authority | Govern | Establish responsibility, escalation, and risk tolerance |
| Identify affected people and consequences | Map | Define context, impacts, and foreseeable harm |
| Validate accuracy, fairness, privacy, safety, and explainability | Measure | Establish metrics, testing, uncertainty, and independent review |
| Remediate blockers and monitor operation | Manage | Prioritize, respond, document, and track residual risk |
| Apply sector overlays | Map + Govern | Tailor risk treatment to operating context |
| Preserve logs and reconstruct decisions | Govern + Measure | Support accountability and incident investigation |
| Provide appeal and remedy | Map + Manage | Connect identified impacts to operational response |

## Distinctive contribution of Rights by Design

Rights by Design makes due process, contestability, remedy, cognitive liberty, democratic control, and deployment-blocker decisions explicit. It can therefore serve as a rights-centered implementation profile alongside AI RMF practices.

## Source
NIST AI 100-1, published January 26, 2023. NIST has stated that AI RMF 1.0 is being revised; verify the current edition before formal use.
