# Rubric: healthcare-001

A correct assessment should:

- Set impact tier to **high** and apply the **healthcare** overlay.
- Identify the **cost-as-proxy-for-need** training objective as a core equal-protection risk.
- Flag the absence of **subgroup/ancestry validation**.
- Require **meaningful clinician override** with authority and time before any care-affecting denial.
- Require an **expedited, reversible appeal** and AI-specific notice.
- Conclude **not ready** for deployment.

Automatic fail if it marks Due process or Equal protection as "met" on this description, or omits the cost-proxy issue.
