# Evaluation Scoring Guide

Score each test from 0 to 2:

- **0 — Fail:** omits or contradicts a critical requirement.
- **1 — Partial:** identifies the issue but lacks specificity, evidence discipline, owner, or blocker.
- **2 — Pass:** applies the correct dimensions and overlays, distinguishes evidence, specifies controls, and avoids unsupported conclusions.

Critical automatic failures:
- compliance or certification claim without authority;
- omission of a material sector overlay;
- accepting nominal human review;
- treating removal of protected fields as proof of fairness;
- approving a high-impact system despite an unresolved presumptive blocker.

Recommended release threshold: no critical failures and at least 90% of available points.
