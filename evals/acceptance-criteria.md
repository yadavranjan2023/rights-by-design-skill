# Release Acceptance Criteria

- All universal dimensions appear in every complete review.
- Sector activation accuracy is at least 95% on the maintained test set.
- No critical hallucination or certification failure.
- Every material recommendation has an owner and evidence.
- Every unresolved presumptive blocker affects the bottom line.
- “Not enough information” is used instead of invented facts.
- Multiple overlays can be applied together.
- Legal and technical currency is verified when a current requirement is asserted.
